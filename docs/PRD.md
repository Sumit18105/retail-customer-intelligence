# Product Requirements Document
## Retail Customer Intelligence & Campaign Analytics

### Product overview
A lightweight analytics application that turns retail transactions into customer intelligence, retention insights and campaign recommendations.

### Goals
- Working end-to-end analytics MVP
- RFM segmentation
- Business KPIs and trends
- SQL proficiency + query optimization
- Public deployment

### Users
Marketing/engagement managers, data analysts and business managers.

### Must-have requirements
Data validation, KPI calculations, RFM metrics, segments, interactive dashboard, recommended actions, SQL indexing/query plans, public deployment.

### Optional
Natural-language “Ask Your Data”.

### Out of scope
Production cloud warehouse, Airflow, authentication, real-time streaming and complex LLM agents.

### Success criteria
Public URL works; dashboard answers the core business questions; SQL reconciles with source data; high-value/at-risk customers are visible; query-plan demo is reproducible.
